# Design

The game-screen delta removes the stale scenario that says observers may close a purchase or auction dialog. The accepted dialog contract gives purchase decisions only to the decider and auction sheets only to the active responder; observers see non-modal status. The accessibility requirement continues to govern surfaces that actually exist.

The logos delta replaces the ambiguous “visual-design effort summary” reference with the exact durable path `docs/efforts/visual-design/logo-exceptions.md`. No thresholds or exception evidence requirements change.
